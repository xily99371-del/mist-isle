# Mist Isle v3.1 — Gameplay Direction

This document records the agreed direction before implementation. Keep v3.0.x as the stable rollback point.

## Core loop

Explore → gather → prepare at campfire → encounter → weaken or befriend → capture → train/evolve → unlock a seal → defeat its guardian → advance.

## Starter selection

- On the first New Game only, show the three starter companions together before the opening journey.
- Let players inspect element, role, signature skill and field ability before choosing.
- The selected starter joins immediately; the other two remain discoverable later, so the choice is meaningful but not permanently missable.
- Store selection in versioned save data; loading old saves must not reopen the choice or reset ownership.

## Wild encounters, feeding and capture

- Support two routes: weaken a wild creature and use a crafted Resonance Capsule; or offer preferred food and attempt a probabilistic befriending.
- Each species has food affinity, temperament and readable reactions. Show cues such as curious, wary or nearly trusting before the roll.
- Suggested chance model: clamp(base + missing-HP bonus + food affinity + status bonus + capsule quality − temperament resistance, minimum, maximum).
- Bosses and story guardians are not capturable. Failed attempts should teach the player something; tutorial attempts do not consume rare resources.

## Gathering and campfire crafting

- Place berries, dew, shell fragments, glow pollen and crystal dust in authored map locations and optional side paths.
- The campfire becomes a preparation hub for recipes, cooked food and capture capsules.
- Early recipes teach the system; stronger treats/capsules require materials from optional hazards or guardian rewards.
- Recipes are discoverable through the guide and environmental notes, not arbitrary trial-and-error.

## Capture tutorial guide

Introduce an original guide character, provisionally named 拾光, at the coastal camp. The guide demonstrates inspection, feeding, a failed response, weakening, capsule use and a campfire recipe. The tutorial becomes skippable after the first demonstration and does not consume rare materials.

## Companions as exploration partners

- Moss/light: reveal hidden markings, regrow root bridges and soothe frightened creatures.
- Water: fill dry channels, cool hot stones and move floating platforms.
- Crystal/electric: activate conductors, energize sealed mechanisms and break resonant mineral barriers.
- Environmental affordances should hint at the correct partner, avoiding trial-and-error menus.

## Combat, attributes and growth

- Add a small elemental matchup system with a clear battle indicator. Proposed cycle: Nature beats Crystal, Crystal beats Water, Water beats Nature. Preserve wet → electric as a separate tactical interaction.
- Keep pet-only HP, three-pet combat and party-wide healing.
- Add ordinary encounters and one mechanic-focused elite before each seal guardian so players learn the relevant matchup and guard timing.
- Companions gain XP, levels and bond. Evolution changes silhouette, palette accents and at least one skill or field interaction, not just stats.
- The starter evolves first through the chapter climax; other companions evolve through level/bond plus a regional catalyst.

## Seal progression and final gate

- Each seal point has a named guardian. Defeat it, activate the mechanism and show the seal visually breaking.
- Require all three regional resonances and their field interactions before the final lighthouse gate opens. The journal tracks each condition.
- Every guardian teaches a mechanic used later in the lighthouse fight.

## Final boss climax

- At the final threshold, the boss locks the arena and performs a clearly telegraphed story-only resonance pulse that downs the active party. Do not silently set HP to zero during ordinary combat.
- A quiet scene follows. The starter responds to the bond built throughout the chapter and evolves into an awakened form.
- The awakened starter revives the team to a small, explicit HP amount and unlocks a signature counter that makes the final phase winnable. The player still performs the final sequence; evolution is a comeback tool, not an automatic win.

## Implementation order

1. Fix collision and Windows presentation.
2. Add starter selection and save migration.
3. Implement item inventory and campfire recipe data.
4. Implement wild encounters, feeding, capture capsules and tutorial guide.
5. Add field abilities and seal guardian gates.
6. Add elemental matchup UI, encounters, XP/bond and evolution.
7. Implement the final boss downbeat and starter awakening.
8. Balance using first-playthrough timing and usability tests.

Keep gameplay data separate from rendering and battle logic. New progression fields must migrate old saves without losing items, companions, levels or boss state.
