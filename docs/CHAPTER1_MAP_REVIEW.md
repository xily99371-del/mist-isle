## Shared constraints
- All maps remain 18×12 cells with 56px tiles.
- Story markers and interaction coordinates remain stable.
- Main routes are continuous and readable before decoration is placed.
- Buildings, trees and large props frame the route; they do not occupy its centre.
- Terrain transitions use coherent bands and clearings rather than scattered single-cell patches.
- The map compiler and reachability validation remain mandatory after edits.
