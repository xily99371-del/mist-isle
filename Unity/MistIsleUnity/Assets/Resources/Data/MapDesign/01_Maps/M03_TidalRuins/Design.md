# M03 潮汐遗迹 / Tidal Ruins

- Size: 112 × 80 tiles.
- Role: two-subzone puzzle branch; yields Tide Seal and Crystal Seal.
- Exits: WEST_01 ↔ M01.EAST_01; NORTH_01 ↔ M04.SOUTH_01, gated by all three seals.
- Subzones: Tide Temple (water-level switches, channels, floating platforms, 断潮蟹王) and Crystal Archive (energy conduits, crystal puzzles, 棱晶蛇王).
- Guardians: 断潮蟹王 grants `tide_seal`; 棱晶蛇王 grants `crystal_seal`.
